Korean War Brides Project

Documenting the lives of Korean women who married American servicemen during and after the Korean War — in their own voices, and through the records they left behind.

What this project is about

Between 1950 and the decades that followed, tens of thousands of Korean women emigrated to the United States as the wives of American servicemen. They arrived into unfamiliar towns, often as the only Korean person for miles, and built families, businesses, and communities that reshaped Korean America.

Their stories are rarely written down. This project sets out to record them directly — long-form interviews with the women themselves and with their children and grandchildren — and to gather the photographs, letters, and documents that sit in family albums rather than institutional archives.
