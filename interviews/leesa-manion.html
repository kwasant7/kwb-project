Interview with Leesa Manion: A Life of Loss, Reunion, and Advocacy

Interviewed September 4, 2026 | 23 minutes

Leesa Manion talks about her parents’ meeting in Korea, growing up biracial in Kentucky after her mother was forced from the family home, finding her mother again as an adult, and how that loss shaped her.

Background

Leesa Manion was born near Seoul in April 1969 to a Korean mother and an American soldier, and moved as an infant to Covington, Kentucky, where her mother was the only Asian person in the neighborhood. When Leesa was four, her mother was forced out of the family home and out of her children’s lives. Leesa grew up biracial with little connection to her Korean heritage, became a lawyer, and found her mother again 25 years later. Her mother’s strength, and what it cost her to lose her children, shaped Leesa’s empathy for people without power.
