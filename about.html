Method

Interviews are recorded in person where possible and remotely where not. Narrators review their own interview before it is published, and may withdraw or restrict any part of it at any time. Recordings are published in full rather than edited into a narrative, so that the pacing and digressions of each account are preserved.

A note on language

"War bride" is the term used in the immigration records and press of the period, and it is the phrase most narrators recognize. It is used here for that reason, with an awareness that it was not always a term the women chose for themselves.
